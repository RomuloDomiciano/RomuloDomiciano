## 🤖 What I focus on

* Building **AI agents and agentic workflows**
* Designing prompts, reasoning flows, and tool-calling strategies
* Connecting LLMs to APIs, databases, documents, and enterprise systems
* Developing **Retrieval-Augmented Generation — RAG** solutions
* Creating backend services and AI integrations with Python
* Designing secure, observable, and scalable AI architectures
* Taking solutions from discovery and prototyping to deployment
