## 🧠 How I approach engineering

```text
          Business problem
                 |
                 v
         Technical discovery
                 |
                 v
      Architecture & experimentation
                 |
                 v
      Software, AI & integrations
                 |
                 v
       Validation & observability
                 |
                 v
          Production delivery
                 |
                 v
        Measurable business impact
```

I enjoy working on problems where the solution is not completely defined yet.

My role is often to understand the context, reduce ambiguity, design the architecture, build the solution, and help turn an idea into a reliable product.

**My engineering principles:**

- Problem first, technology second
- Deterministic when possible, AI when valuable
- Build simple, evolve intelligently
- Human judgment where it matters
- Experiment quickly, validate continuously
- Design for reliability, observability, and maintainability

> Great engineering is not about choosing the most sophisticated technology. It's about building the right solution for the problem.
